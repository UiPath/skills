Usage: uip ah [options] [command]

Automation Hub — automation ideas, pipeline, taxonomy and users.

Commands:
  applications   Manage the application inventory
  auth-info      Show the Automation Hub tenant and your identity in it
  automations    Work with automation ideas
  categories     Read the category tree
  documents      Manage the documents attached to an automation
  idea-flows     Discover the configured idea flows
  phases         Move an automation through the pipeline
  users          List tenant users
