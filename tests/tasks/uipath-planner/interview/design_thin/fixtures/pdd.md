# Process Definition Document: Supplier Invoice Entry

## Purpose

Accounts Payable at Contoso enters supplier invoices into the ERP by hand. The team wants the entry automated.

## As-is steps

1. An invoice arrives from the supplier.
2. A clerk reads the supplier, the invoice number, the invoice date, the purchase order number and the total.
3. The clerk checks the invoice against the purchase order.
4. An invoice over the limit needs approval before it is posted.
5. The clerk posts the invoice in the ERP.
6. An invoice that cannot be matched is set aside.

## Systems

- Contoso ERP
- E-mail

## Notes

- Invoices are PDF files, in a different layout per supplier.
