## Recommended Scope

**Recommendation:** SINGLE_PRODUCT — Case Management
**Delivery model:** cloud
**Blocked by platform:** none
**Need profile:** A claim sits in an intake hold. Separately, the claimant may withdraw the
claim at any point, which arrives as an inbound event from the Acme Claims intake webhook
connector and diverts the case into a withdrawal lane.

### Stage 1: Intake Hold

**Type:** Stage
**Stage Kind:** primary
**Design Rationale:** The case needs one required milestone so `required-stages-completed` has something to key off. A short timer stands in for the intake dwell.
**Description:** Holds the claim briefly during intake.
**Required for Case Completion:** Yes

| case-entered | — | No | Case started |

#### Stage Exit Conditions

|------|-----|-----------|---------------------|--------------|
| required-tasks-completed | — | exit-only | Yes | Intake hold done |

#### Tasks

| # | Task Name | Type | Activation Mode | Starts When | Required | Run Only Once | Persona | SLA |
|---|-----------|------|-----------------|-------------|----------|---------------|---------|-----|
| 1 | Hold Briefly | wait-for-timer | parallel | stage enters | Yes | No | system | — |

##### Task 1.1: Hold Briefly

**Type:** wait-for-timer
**Activation Mode:** parallel
**Design Rationale:** A deterministic, resource-free dwell — it needs no tenant registry entry, keeping the case's only unresolved resource the withdrawal connector.
**Description:** Waits a few seconds to represent the intake dwell.

### Stage 2: Withdrawal Received

**Type:** Stage
**Stage Kind:** secondary
**Design Rationale:** Withdrawal can arrive at any point and is not part of the happy path, so it is a non-required secondary lane rather than a step in the main flow.
**Description:** Handles a claim the claimant withdrew mid-intake.
**Required for Case Completion:** No

#### Stage Entry Conditions

| WHEN | IF | Interrupting | Display Name |
|------|-----|-------------|--------------|
| wait-for-connector | — | Yes | Withdrawal received |

**Connector Detail for the entry rule:**

**Auth Method:** Connection-managed
