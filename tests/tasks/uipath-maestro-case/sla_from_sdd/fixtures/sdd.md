### Case-Level SLA Escalation Rules

**Design Rationale:** The case-level clock is a reporting boundary, not a routing one — the source describes no case-wide graph change, so both statuses stay notifications and every acting response lives at stage scope. At-risk is 70% per the 3–10 day band.

| SLA Status | Threshold | Action | Display Name |
|------------|-----------|--------|--------------|
| At-Risk | 70% of SLA duration | Notify: Role Claims Leads | Claim resolution at risk |
| Breached | 100% of SLA duration | Notify: Role Claims Leads | Claim resolution breached |

### Variable SLA Rules

> None.

### SLA Response Map

**Design Rationale:** One row per (scope, SLA, status). The two acting responses are deliberately
different shapes: Intake's breach starts a task inside the breached stage, while Assessment's breach
hands the claim to a separate lane. Everything else notifies.

| Scope | SLA | Status | Response | Target | Interrupting | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| case | Claim resolution SLA | At-Risk | notify-only | — | — | Reporting boundary; no case-wide graph change described. |
| case | Claim resolution SLA | Breached | notify-only | — | — | Same; the acting responses live at stage scope. |
| stage: Intake | Intake SLA | At-Risk | notify-only | — | — | Warning only, so a handler can act before the deadline. |
| stage: Intake | Intake SLA | Breached | start-task | Chase Missing Paperwork | — | Chasing paperwork is intake work, so the follow-up lives in the breached stage. |
| stage: Assessment | Assessment SLA | At-Risk | notify-only | — | — | Warning only. |
| stage: Assessment | Assessment SLA | Breached | enter-stage | Escalation Review | Yes | A lead takes the claim over, so the lane interrupts the assessor's work. |
| stage: Settlement | Settlement SLA | At-Risk | notify-only | — | — | Warning only. |
| stage: Settlement | Settlement SLA | Breached | notify-only | — | — | No work on breach is described. |

### Stage 1: Intake

**Type:** Stage
**Design Rationale:** A primary stage because every claim passes through validation before an assessor spends time on it. Its breach response is a follow-up task inside this same stage rather than a new lane, because the source describes chasing paperwork as part of intake work.
**Description:** Validates the claim details and records whether the paperwork is complete enough to assess.
**Required for case completion:** Yes

#### Stage Entry Conditions

| WHEN | IF | Interrupting | Display Name |

| case-entered | — | No | Claim received |

#### Stage Exit Conditions

| required-tasks-completed | — | exit-only | Yes | Intake complete |

#### Stage SLA

**SLA Type:** time-based
**SLA Title:** Intake SLA
**Duration:** 1 d

**Design Rationale:** One day to validate a claim. A breach starts a chase task inside this stage — the assessor is not involved yet, so nothing is handed over and no lane is entered.

### Stage 2: Assessment

**Type:** Stage
**Design Rationale:** A primary stage because every validated claim is assessed. Its SLA is condition-based: high-value claims carry a shorter clock, and a breach hands the claim to a separate escalation lane rather than adding work here, because the source describes a different team taking it over.
**Description:** Assesses the damage and takes the settle-or-reject decision.
**Required for case completion:** Yes

#### Stage Entry Conditions

| WHEN | IF | Interrupting | Display Name |
|------|-----|--------------|--------------|
| selected-stage-completed("Intake") | =js:(vars.paperworkComplete === true) | No | Paperwork complete |

#### Stage Exit Conditions

| WHEN | IF | Exit Type | Marks Stage Complete | Display Name |
|------|-----|-----------|---------------------|--------------|
| required-tasks-completed | =js:(vars.assessorDecision !== "Reject") | exit-only | Yes | Assessment complete |
| selected-tasks-completed("Assessor Decision") | =js:(vars.assessorDecision === "Reject") | exit-only | No | Rejected by assessor |

#### Stage SLA

**SLA Type:** condition-based
**SLA Title:** Assessment SLA
**Duration:** 2 d

**Design Rationale:** Two days for a standard claim, four hours when the claimed amount is over 5000 — the source treats high-value claims as time-critical. A breach enters the escalation lane, which takes the claim over, so that entry interrupts.

#### Stage Conditional SLA Rules

| Expression | SLA | Unit | Display Name |
|------------|-----|------|--------------|
| =js:(vars.claimValue > 5000) | 4 | h | Assessment SLA high value |

| SLA Status | Threshold | Action | Display Name |
|------------|-----------|--------|--------------|
| At-Risk | 75% of SLA duration | Notify: Role Claims Handlers | Assessment at risk |
| Breached | 100% of SLA duration | Notify: Role Claims Leads | Assessment breached |

### Secondary Stage: Escalation Review

**Type:** Stage
**Stage Kind:** secondary
**Design Rationale:** A secondary lane because an assessment that misses its clock is handed to a lead, not worked on further by the assessor. Entered by the Assessment SLA breach and interrupting, because the lead takes the claim over; it returns to origin so the assessor resumes once unblocked.
**Description:** A claims lead reviews an assessment that missed its deadline and hands it back.
**Required for case completion:** No
**Interrupting:** Yes
