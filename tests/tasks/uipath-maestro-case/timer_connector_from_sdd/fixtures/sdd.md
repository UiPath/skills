## Recommended Scope

**Product:** Maestro Case Management (single product).

**Rationale:** A scheduled, long-running compliance sweep with human-visible stages, an
externally-signalled wait, and a hold period before disposition. The work is stage-shaped and
spans days, which is case behaviour rather than a single orchestration run.

### Case-Level SLA Escalation Rules

**Design Rationale:** The sweep clock is a reporting boundary. The source describes no routing change on a missed deadline, so both statuses stay notifications and no acting response is invented.

| SLA Status | Threshold | Action | Display Name |
|------------|-----------|--------|--------------|
| At-Risk | 75% of SLA duration | Notify: Role Compliance Leads | Sweep at risk |
| Breached | 100% of SLA duration | Notify: Role Compliance Leads | Sweep breached |
