# Amendment 2 to the Travel Expense Reimbursement Policy, Version 2.0
Effective January 1, 2027. Issued by Finance.
## Changes
Section 5 is replaced with: "A receipt is required for every single expense above $20. An expense above $20 without a receipt is rejected."
Section 7, first sentence, is replaced with: "A claim must be submitted within 45 days after the end of the trip."
All other sections are unchanged.
