# Travel Expense Reimbursement Policy
Version 2.0, effective January 1, 2026. Issued by Finance.
## 1. Scope
This policy applies to all employees who travel on company business.
## 2. Airfare
Airfare must be booked in economy class.
Notwithstanding the previous sentence, a flight longer than 6 hours may be booked in premium economy.
Airfare above $800 requires written pre-approval from the employee's manager; without it the expense is rejected.
Employees should book airfare at least 14 days before departure.
## 3. Lodging
Hotel cost is reimbursed up to $200 per night for domestic travel.
Hotel cost is reimbursed up to $300 per night for international travel.
A hotel cost above the nightly limit is rejected.
<!-- page: 2 -->
## 4. Meals
Meals are reimbursed up to $60 per day for domestic travel and $90 per day for international travel.
Alcohol is never reimbursed.
## 5. Receipts
A receipt is required for every single expense above $25.
An expense above $25 without a receipt is rejected.
## 6. Vehicles
Employees may use a personal vehicle for business travel.
Mileage is reimbursed at the rate in the Rate Schedule.
## 7. Submission
A claim must be submitted within 30 days after the end of the trip.
A claim submitted after 30 days is referred to Finance for review.
If information needed to decide an expense is missing, the claim is referred to Finance for review.
