# SOP: Reviewing a Travel Expense Claim
Owner: Finance Operations. This procedure describes how reviewers handle claims; the Travel Expense Reimbursement Policy governs what is reimbursed.
## 1. Steps
1. Open the claim in the expense tool and confirm the trip end date.
2. Check each line against the policy limits.
3. Mark each line Approved, Rejected, or Referred.
4. Add a note to every Rejected or Referred line.
## 2. Service level
Reviewers aim to decide a claim within 3 business days.
## 3. Escalation
A claim whose total is above $5,000 is escalated to the Director before payment.
## 4. Reviewer practice
Reviewers typically accept hotel costs up to $250 per night for domestic trips to high-cost cities.
## 5. Worked examples
Example A: A domestic hotel at $190 per night with a receipt is Approved.
Example B: A domestic hotel at $240 per night in a high-cost city is Approved.
Example C: A $30 taxi fare without a receipt is Rejected.
Example D: An international flight at $950 with no manager pre-approval is Rejected.
