# Travel Expense FAQ
Maintained by Finance Operations. Answers are guidance for employees.
## Questions
Q: Is Canada international travel?
A: Canada and Mexico are treated as domestic travel for lodging and meals.
Q: Does the alcohol rule cover the hotel minibar?
A: Yes. Minibar alcohol is not reimbursed.
Q: Can my manager approve airfare up to $1,000 verbally?
A: Yes. Managers can approve airfare up to $1,000 verbally, so written pre-approval is not needed below that amount.
