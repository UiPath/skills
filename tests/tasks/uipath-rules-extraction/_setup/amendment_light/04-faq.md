# Travel Expense FAQ
Maintained by Finance Operations. Answers are guidance for employees.
## Questions
Q: Can a manager approve a hotel over the limit verbally?
A: Yes. Managers can approve a hotel up to $250 per night verbally, so the nightly limit does not apply below that amount.
Q: Are meals reimbursed on travel days?
A: Yes. The daily meal limit applies on travel days too.
