# Remote Work Equipment Reimbursement Policy
Version 3.0, effective January 1 of the current fiscal year.
## 1. Eligibility
An employee is eligible for reimbursement only after completing at least 90 days of service.
## 2. Approval limits
A request of $500 or less is approved.
A request above $500 and up to $2,000 is approved only when the employee has written pre-approval from their director.
A request above $2,000 is denied.
If the amount of the request or the employee's length of service is not stated, the request is referred to the Finance team for review.
<!-- page: 2 -->
## 3. Exceptions
Notwithstanding section 1, an employee with an approved medical accommodation is eligible regardless of length of service.
## 4. Receipts
Employees may submit receipts as a PDF or as a photo.
## 5. Administration
Finance reviews this policy every year.
