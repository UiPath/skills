11	> - References to unknown variables or node IDs
12	> - Output-path walks that descend into declared primitives or schemas closed with `additionalProperties: false`
13	>
14	> Errors against **open output schemas** (HTTP response bodies, script returns, free-text agent output) and **wrong-direction reads** (e.g. reading an `out`-only variable) still slip past validate and surface only at `flow debug` or in production.
