## 📋 CustomerEmailEscalation — Flow Architecture Plan

### What it does

The moment a support email hits your inbox, a UiPath AI agent reads and classifies it as **urgent**, **high-priority**, or **normal**. Based on that classification:

- **Urgent** → Customer gets an immediate reply, the email is forwarded to your escalation team, and it's marked read to prevent double-handling.
- **High Priority** → Customer gets a "within 4 hours" reply, email is moved to a dedicated high-priority folder.
- **Normal** → Email is routed to your standard support queue folder.
