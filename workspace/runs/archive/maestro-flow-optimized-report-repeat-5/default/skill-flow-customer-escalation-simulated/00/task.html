## 📋 EmailTriage — Flow Plan

### What it does
Every time an email arrives in your Outlook inbox, the flow **automatically classifies it as Critical, High, or Normal**, then takes action based on that priority:

- **🔴 Critical** — tagged, moved to a dedicated "Critical" folder, and (optionally) alerts your team via chat
- **🟡 High** — tagged for follow-up
- **🟢 Normal** — tagged and left in place

### How it works

